# Organizations Schema Documentation

An `Organization` represents an institution such as a company or university.

## Detailed Guides

*   [Local Businesses](local-businesses.md) - Physical stores and branches.
*   [Educational Organizations](educational.md) - Schools and Universities.

## Other Types
*   **Corporation**: Business corporations.
*   **NGO**: Non-governmental organizations.
*   **MedicalOrganization**: Hospitals and clinics.
