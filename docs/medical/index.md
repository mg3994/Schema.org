# Medical & Health Schema Documentation

`MedicalEntity` is the root for all medical-related schemas.

## Detailed Guides

*   [Conditions & Diseases](conditions.md) - Infectious diseases, chronic conditions.
*   [Treatments & Procedures](treatments.md) - Drugs, surgeries, therapies.
*   [Life Sciences & BioChem](life-sciences.md) - Genes, Proteins, Taxons.

## Exhaustive Categories

*   **Anatomical Structure / System**: Bones, Muscles, Organs.
*   **Medical Study / Trial**: Clinical research.
*   **Medical Test**: Labs and imaging.

## Tips for Medical Schema
*   **Coding Systems**: Use `MedicalCode` for mapping to ICD-10.
*   **Authority**: Ensure content is professional and reviewed.
